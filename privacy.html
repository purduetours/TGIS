01

Who We Are

The Tour Guide Information System (TGIS) is a student-created and student-operated platform built for and used exclusively by tour guides and management staff within the Purdue University Office of Admissions Tour Guide Program.

TGIS is not an official Purdue University product and is maintained by the student directors of the Ambassador Program.

02

Who This Applies To

This policy applies exclusively to active and former members of the Purdue Tour Guide Program. TGIS is not accessible to or intended for the general public, prospective students, or anyone outside the Tour Guide Program.

03

What Information We Collect

When you use TGIS, the following categories of information are collected and retained.

Account & Identity

Your name, Purdue username, Purdue email address, PUID, and role within the program.

Profile Information

Information you provide as part of program onboarding and census forms, including graduation date, academic major, college, hometown, and dietary preferences where voluntarily submitted.

Tour & Schedule Data

Records of tours assigned to you, tours you have given, and your tour history within the program.

Usage Information

When you access TGIS, a record of your login — including your username, the date and time of access, and which part of the platform you accessed — is retained for administrative purposes.

04

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for the operation of the Tour Guide Program. Specifically:

To display your schedule, tour history, and program statistics to you
To allow program managers to coordinate tour assignments and track participation
To send you program-related notifications and reminders
To calculate and display family group standings and program milestones
To fulfill administrative requirements of the Tour Guide Program

Your information is never sold, shared with third parties for marketing, or used for any purpose outside the Tour Guide Program.

05

Notifications & Messaging

TGIS integrates with Slack to deliver program notifications to your Purdue-affiliated account. These notifications may include tour assignment confirmations, milestone achievements, census reminders, and end-of-semester summaries.

Notifications are sent to the Slack account associated with your Purdue email address. You may manage your notification preferences within Slack directly.

06

Data Retention

Your information is retained for the duration of your participation in the Tour Guide Program and for a reasonable period thereafter for historical record-keeping purposes. Former guides' tour history is maintained as part of the program's permanent records.

If you would like to request removal of your personal information following your departure from the program, you may contact the student directors directly.

07

Data Security

Access to TGIS and the data it contains is restricted to active tour guides and program management only. Administrative functions require additional authentication. We take reasonable precautions to protect your information from unauthorized access.

08

Your Rights

As a user of TGIS you have the right to:

Access the personal information the program holds about you
Request corrections to inaccurate information
Request removal of your personal information following your departure from the program

To exercise any of these rights, contact the student directors of the Ambassador Program directly.

09

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time as the program evolves. Material changes will be communicated through the usual program channels. Continued use of TGIS following any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10

Terms of Use

By accessing and using TGIS you agree to the following.

✓You will only access TGIS using your own credentials and will not share access with others
✓You will use the platform solely for legitimate Tour Guide Program purposes
✓You will not attempt to access, modify, or extract data belonging to other users
✓You understand that your activity within the platform is logged for administrative purposes
✓You will report any suspected security issues or unauthorized access to the student directors immediately

Misuse of the platform may result in removal of access and referral to the appropriate program management or university office.

11

Contact

Questions about this policy or requests regarding your personal data should be directed to the student directors of the Purdue University Tour Guide Ambassador Program through the standard program communication channels.
