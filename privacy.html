01

Who We Are

The Tour Guide Information System (TGIS) is a student-created and student-operated platform built for and used exclusively by tour guides and management staff (collectively, "End Users") within the Purdue University Office of Admissions Ambassador Program ("the Program"). TGIS is not an official product of Purdue University and is developed, maintained, and operated solely by the student directors of the Ambassador Program. By accessing or using TGIS in any capacity, End Users acknowledge and agree to the terms set forth in this Policy.

02

Who This Applies To

This Policy applies exclusively to current and former End Users of the Program. TGIS is an internal operational platform and is not accessible to, nor intended for, the general public, prospective students, families, or any individual outside the Program. Access is provisioned solely at the discretion of Program management.

03

What Information We Collect

TGIS collects and retains the following categories of information in connection with the operation and administration of the Program. End Users are informed of this collection as a condition of participation.

Account & Identity

Your provided name, Purdue-issued username, Purdue email address, Purdue University identification number (PUID), and designated role classification within the Program.

Profile Information

Biographical, academic, and term-specific information voluntarily provided by the End User through Program onboarding and census processes. This includes, but is not limited to, information pertaining to the End User's academic standing, enrollment status, and Program-specific preferences. The specific data fields collected through census processes are documented within those forms at the time of submission. Disclosure of what specific data fields are retained beyond what appears within the Platform is limited for the purposes of system integrity and access security.

Tour & Schedule Data

A complete record of tour assignments, tour activity, and cumulative tour history associated with the End User's account for the duration of their participation in the Program.

Usage Information

A record is maintained of all access to the Platform, including but not limited to: username, timestamp, source interface, and the outcome of the access attempt. All page access attempts — whether successful or unsuccessful — are recorded. Attempts to access restricted pages for which the End User does not hold the appropriate role authorization are automatically flagged by the system and may result in immediate revocation of Platform access without prior notice.

04

How We Use Your Information

Information collected by TGIS is used exclusively for the lawful administration and operation of the Program. No information collected by TGIS is sold, licensed, disclosed to third parties for commercial purposes, or used for any purpose unrelated to the Program. Specific operational uses include:

To display your schedule, tour history, and program statistics to you
To allow program managers to coordinate tour assignments and track participation
To send you program-related notifications and reminders
To calculate and display family group standings and program milestones
To fulfill administrative requirements of the Tour Guide Program

Any use of End User information beyond the purposes enumerated above requires explicit authorization from Program management.

05

Notifications & Messaging

TGIS integrates with Slack to facilitate the delivery of Program-related notifications to End Users' Purdue-affiliated Slack accounts. In order to deliver such communications, TGIS retrieves certain information associated with the End User's Slack account, including but not limited to the End User's Slack username, Purdue email address, Slack member ID, and Slack profile ID. This information is used solely to identify and contact the appropriate recipient and is not retained beyond what is necessary to transmit the notification.

TGIS utilizes a large language model to monitor and process messages posted in designated tour tracking channels, specifically the channels known as #subrequests and #specialrequests within the Program's Slack workspace. All messages posted in these channels are subject to automated processing by the model for the purpose of updating the tour schedule. End Users should be aware that messages posted in these channels may be processed by the model operator and may in turn be used by that operator for training or service improvement purposes in accordance with the operator's own terms of service. TGIS does not access, monitor, read, or process messages from any End User's personal direct messages, or from any Slack channel other than those expressly identified above.

End Users may manage their Slack notification preferences within the Slack platform directly. Certain Program notifications are considered operationally essential and may not be suppressible through Slack settings alone.

06

Data Retention

End User information is retained for the duration of active participation in the Program. Upon departure from the Program — whether voluntary or otherwise — the End User's profile and associated personal information shall be retained for a period of ninety (90) days, after which it may be purged from the active system at the discretion of Program management. Tour history and cumulative performance records may be retained indefinitely as part of the Program's permanent historical records, independent of the End User's active participation status.

Requests for early removal of personal information may be submitted to the student directors of the Program and will be considered on a case-by-case basis. The Program reserves the right to retain records required for administrative, audit, or historical purposes notwithstanding any such request.

07

Data Security

Access to TGIS and the data contained therein is restricted to provisioned End Users and Program management only. Certain pages and administrative functions within the Platform are further restricted based on the End User's assigned role classification and require additional authentication in addition to standard access credentials. All access activity — including unauthorized access attempts — is logged automatically.

Any attempt by an End User to access a page, function, or profile for which they do not hold the requisite authorization will be automatically flagged by the system. Similarly, any attempt to access another End User's profile or account information without authorization constitutes a violation of this Policy and will be flagged accordingly. Either category of violation may result in the immediate and permanent revocation of Platform access and referral to appropriate Program management or university personnel.

The Program takes reasonable and appropriate technical precautions to protect End User information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. However, as with any digital system, absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

08

Your Rights

As a user of TGIS you have the right to:

Access the personal information the program holds about you
Request corrections to inaccurate information
Request removal of your personal information following your departure from the program

To exercise any of these rights, contact the student directors of the Ambassador Program directly.

09

Changes to This Policy

The Program reserves the right to amend this Policy at any time. Material amendments will be communicated to End Users through standard Program communication channels. Continued access to or use of TGIS following the publication of any amendment constitutes the End User's acceptance of the revised Policy. End Users who do not agree to the revised terms should discontinue use of the Platform and notify the student directors accordingly.

10

Terms of Use

By accessing or using TGIS, the End User agrees to be bound by the following terms. Violation of any of these terms may result in the immediate revocation of Platform access and, where appropriate, referral to Program management or relevant university offices.

✓You will only access TGIS using your own credentials and will not share access with others
✓You will use the platform solely for legitimate Tour Guide Program purposes
✓You will not attempt to access, modify, or extract data belonging to other users
✓You understand that your activity within the platform is logged for administrative purposes
✓You will report any suspected security issues or unauthorized access to the student directors immediately

These terms are in addition to, and do not supersede, any applicable Purdue University policies, codes of conduct, or other governing agreements to which the End User may be subject.

11

Contact

Questions about this policy or requests regarding your personal data should be directed to the student directors of the Purdue University Tour Guide Ambassador Program through the standard program communication channels.
